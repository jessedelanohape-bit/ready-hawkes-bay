    How to pack a grab bag

    1. Use a backpack so your hands are free. Each person carries a bag they can manage for an hour of walking.
    2. Heavy things (water, food) go in the middle, close to your back. Soft clothes go around them.
    3. Seal documents, medicine and electronics in zip-lock or dry bags. Flooding and rain are likely.
    4. Torch, first aid kit and a snack go in the top or outside pocket.
    5. Label each bag with the person's name and your out-of-region contact.
    6. Keep the bags by the door you would leave from, with walking shoes beside them.

    Keep it ready
